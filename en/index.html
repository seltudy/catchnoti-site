FAQ

Can I see notifications from before I installed it?

No. CatchNoti keeps notifications that arrive after you turn on the automation.

I can't find a deleted message

The message may not have come as a notification, for example if you were viewing that chat or had notifications off. If message previews are off inside a messenger like KakaoTalk, notifications are kept without their text. When notifications arrive within about a second of each other, some may be missed. Check Settings › Capture Status.

What does 'some may be missing' mean?

When several notifications arrive within about a second, iOS doesn't pass some of them to CatchNoti. This mark appears where a notification arrived very soon after the previous one. Check missed notifications in the original app.

Are photos kept too?

Only the text shown in the notification is kept. Photos and emoticons appear as 'Photo' and 'Emoticon' labels.

Are my messages uploaded to a server?

No. They're stored only on this iPhone, and the developer can't see them. If iCloud Backup is on, they're included in your device backup like other app data.

Are ads chosen by reading my messages?

No. Notification content, your app list, and searches are never sent when ads are requested. If you allow app tracking, the advertising identifier is used to pick ads that match your interests; otherwise you see general ads. With Pro, no ads appear.

Does reading in CatchNoti mark messages as read?

No. The original app isn't opened, so no read receipt is left.

A messenger is missing from the Chats tab

Turn the app on in Settings › Apps in Chats. Notifications saved earlier are grouped into chats too.

Notifications from a newly installed app aren't saved

Open the CatchNoti automation in Shortcuts › Automation and add the app to its app list.

What is the daily notification from CatchNoti?

It's a check that saving still works. Once it's saved, it disappears right away. If it stays, saving has stopped. You can change the time in Settings › Capture Status.

Does Low Power Mode or a Focus cause missed notifications?

No. Notifications are kept as they arrive, even with Low Power Mode, a Focus, or Scheduled Summary on.

Notifications were missed after I restarted my phone

After a restart, iOS doesn't run automations until you unlock the first time, so notifications in between aren't kept. Once you unlock, new notifications are kept again.

After changing the language, 'Check Now' fails or notifications are missed

Right after you change the iPhone language, iOS doesn't pass notification content to Shortcuts for a few minutes. Notifications in that time may be missed. After a few minutes, tap 'Check Now' again in Settings › Capture Status.

What happens to my records if I delete the app?

They're deleted with it. Device backups such as iCloud Backup include them like other app data.

I deleted the Shortcuts app

Download Shortcuts again from the App Store, then follow Settings › Capture Status › Set Up Again from the Start.
