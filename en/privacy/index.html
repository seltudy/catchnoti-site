Privacy Policy

Seltudy makes and runs CatchNoti, an iPhone app. This policy explains what information CatchNoti handles and what personal information Seltudy receives.

Effective November 2, 2026

At a glance

  • Your notification history is stored only on this iPhone. CatchNoti never sends it anywhere, and Seltudy can't see it.
  • CatchNoti has no accounts or analytics tools. The free version shows Google ads, but your notifications are never used for ads.
  • The only personal information Seltudy receives is email you send us.

1. Information that stays on your iPhone

CatchNoti stores the following only on your iPhone and never sends it to Seltudy or anyone else.

  • The app name, bundle ID, title, subtitle, body, and arrival time of each notification your Shortcuts automation passes to CatchNoti
  • Your settings, such as exclusion rules, keyword alert words, how long to keep notifications, and App Lock, plus daily check records

Records are deleted automatically after the period you choose, and you can delete them anytime in Settings › Data. Deleting the app deletes them too. If iCloud Backup or another iOS device backup is on, they're included in the backup like other app data, which Apple manages under your Apple Account and Apple's policies.

Face ID and Touch ID for App Lock are handled by iOS. CatchNoti never receives that information.

2. Network requests CatchNoti makes

CatchNoti makes only three kinds of requests over the internet. None go through Seltudy: icon lookups and purchases go to Apple, and ads come from Google.

App icons
To show an app's icon, CatchNoti sends that app's bundle ID and a country code to Apple's app search (itunes.apple.com). Notification content is never sent. Turn off Settings › General › Download App Icons to stop these requests.
Ads (free version)
CatchNoti gets the ads shown in the chat and notification lists from Google. Notification content, your app list, and searches are never sent. See section 3.
In-app purchase
The App Store handles buying and restoring CatchNoti Pro. Seltudy doesn't receive the buyer's name, contact details, or payment information.

3. Ads in the free version and personalized ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads at the top of the chat and notification lists and between their rows. Google chooses and serves the ads, and Seltudy can't tell who saw which ad. With CatchNoti Pro, no more ads are loaded, and the ads SDK isn't started after that.

What Google receives
Your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), the advertising identifier and app- or developer-scoped device identifiers, the ads you see and tap, usage data such as app launches and taps, performance data such as app launch time, and crash logs and other diagnostic data. These are the items Google discloses for its ads SDK, and the privacy section of our App Store page lists the same items.
What is never sent
Notification content, the list of apps that sent notifications, and your searches are never included in ad requests.
Personalized ads
After setup, CatchNoti asks for permission to track. If you allow it, Google uses the advertising identifier and your activity in other apps and websites to pick ads that match your interests. If you don't, you see non-personalized ads in the same places.
How to opt out
Turn off CatchNoti in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking to stop use of the advertising identifier. Where consent is required, such as in Europe, you choose in Google's consent form at first and can change it anytime in CatchNoti's Settings › Ad Privacy Options.
Google's privacy practices
Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.
Transfer outside Korea
When ads are requested, this information is sent over the network to Google LLC (in the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers). It's used to show and measure ads and to prevent invalid clicks, and kept under Google's policies. Pro removes ad requests entirely; turning off tracking only limits what's used for personalized ads.

4. Personal information Seltudy receives: support email

When you email us, Seltudy receives that email. This is the only personal information Seltudy handles.

Purpose
Only to answer your question and solve the problem.
What we receive
Your email address, the name shown in your email, and your message. If you turn on Include Diagnostics in Settings › Contact Us, the email also includes the app and iOS versions, device model, language, capture status, number of saved notifications, check and blank-run counts for the last 7 days, and the retention period. It never includes notification content or your app list, and you can edit or remove it before sending.
How long we keep it
3 years from our reply, then we delete it. Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce requires records of consumer complaints and dispute handling to be kept for 3 years.
Deletion
When the period ends, we delete the email without delay, including from the trash, so it can't be recovered.
Sharing
We don't share it with anyone, except where a law specifically requires it or an investigative agency requests it under the law.

Processing and transfer outside Korea

We receive and store support email with Gmail by Google LLC.

Recipient
Google LLC (privacy contact: support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form)
Countries
The United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
When and how
Sent over the network when you email us
Items
Email address, name, message, and diagnostics if included
Purpose and retention
Receiving and storing support email, for the period above
How to refuse
If you don't want this transfer, don't email us. We then can't answer your question.

5. Usage statistics and crash reports

If Share With App Developers is on in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple shares crash reports and usage statistics with Seltudy in a form that doesn't identify you. You can turn this off anytime.

6. Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information in your emails, or to stop its processing, at any time. Email us and we'll handle it without delay. A legal representative or someone you authorize can also ask on your behalf.
  • Seltudy can't see the records on your iPhone. You can view and delete them in CatchNoti.

7. Children under 14

Seltudy doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn an email came from a child, we confirm consent from a legal guardian or, if we can't, delete it without delay.

8. How information is protected

  • Records on your iPhone are stored in CatchNoti's app storage, which iOS Data Protection protects. With App Lock on, CatchNoti asks for Face ID or your device passcode when it opens.
  • Support email is kept in an email account that only Seltudy's representative can access.
